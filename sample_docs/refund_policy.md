# Lumora Cloud — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Lumora Cloud is a fictional company used for this demo.

## Monthly plans

Customers on a monthly plan can cancel at any time from the Billing page. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no partial-month refunds are issued for monthly plans.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, customers who cancel an annual plan receive a prorated refund for the unused full months, minus a 10% early-termination fee.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Lumora Cloud does not issue refunds as account credit unless the customer asks for it.

## Service credits for outages

If monthly uptime falls below the 99.9% commitment in the Service Level Agreement, customers can request a service credit. Credits are 10% of the monthly fee for uptime between 99.0% and 99.9%, and 25% for uptime below 99.0%. Credit requests must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
