# Lumora Cloud — Security and Data Handling

## Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are rotated every 90 days and are stored in a hardware security module.

## Data retention

Backups are kept for 35 days. When an account is closed, customer data is permanently deleted 60 days after closure, unless a legal hold applies. Customers can export all of their data from the Settings page at any time before deletion.

## Data residency

Customers on the Business and Enterprise plans can choose to store data in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Oregon) region. Starter-plan data is stored in the US region only.

## Access control

Staff access to production systems requires hardware security keys and is logged. Lumora Cloud supports single sign-on through SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect on the Enterprise plan.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Security issues can be reported to the security team through the responsible-disclosure form on the Trust page. The team acknowledges reports within 2 business days.
